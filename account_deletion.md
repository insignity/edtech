Delete Your LingLoop Account

If you would like to permanently delete your LingLoop account and associated data, follow these
steps:

Open the LingLoop app.
Go to Profile → Delete Account.
Confirm the deletion.

If you no longer have access to the app, contact us at:

ayarsen1995@gmail.com

Data that will be deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

Profile information
Email address
Learning progress
Speaking recordings
AI feedback
Personal settings
Data that may be retained

Some information may be retained for up to 30 days for backup, fraud prevention, or legal
obligations. After this period, it is permanently deleted unless a longer retention period is
required by law.
